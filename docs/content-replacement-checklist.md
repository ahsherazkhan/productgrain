# ProductGrain content replacement checklist

Use this checklist before public launch. The website must never invent names, customers, employment relationships, screenshots, quotations, results, awards, offices, ratings, or permissions. Keep capability-led fallback content visible until verified proof is ready.

For every supplied asset, record its owner, approval date, source file/link, allowed uses, geographic/channel restrictions, expiry (if any), and who may revoke permission.

## Launch-critical company information

- [ ] Final brand name and approved wordmark. The brand should remain centralized in site configuration.
- [ ] Production domain and canonical URL.
- [ ] Public enquiry email and optional telephone number.
- [ ] Correct company/legal entity name.
- [ ] Registered/business address only if the owner intends and is legally required to publish it; do not create a marketing “office” location.
- [ ] Company registration, VAT/tax, or other footer details where legally required and professionally confirmed.
- [ ] Default market and geographic availability statement.
- [ ] UK, US, and fallback booking links.
- [ ] Approved LinkedIn, Instagram, and Facebook links; hide empty networks.
- [ ] Final email sender name/address and internal lead recipient.
- [ ] Service descriptions, timelines, starting prices, and scope caveats approved by the delivery owner.

## Founder

- [ ] Full name.
- [ ] Professional title.
- [ ] Short biography written in direct, verifiable language.
- [ ] Years of experience, with a source/verification note.
- [ ] Selected areas of expertise.
- [ ] High-resolution professional photograph plus a correctly cropped web version.
- [ ] Descriptive alt text based on what the image shows.
- [ ] LinkedIn profile URL.
- [ ] Introduction video, if used.
- [ ] Video poster image.
- [ ] Reviewed caption/subtitle file and transcript.
- [ ] Public email/contact route.
- [ ] Market-appropriate booking link.
- [ ] Written approval for use across the site, social previews, organic social, and paid advertising as applicable.

If no approved photograph exists, use the designed initial-based avatar. Do not substitute stock photography or an AI-generated person.

## Team

For each person:

- [ ] Full name.
- [ ] Accurate role and employment/engagement relationship.
- [ ] Professional photograph or approval to use an initial-based avatar.
- [ ] Short biography.
- [ ] LinkedIn profile URL.
- [ ] Publication status.
- [ ] Written permission for the supplied text and image.
- [ ] Alt text.
- [ ] Date checked for accuracy.

Do not describe contractors as permanent in-house employees without confirmation. Hide unpublished profiles cleanly.

## Case studies

For each proposed project:

- [ ] Internal working title and public title.
- [ ] Client name or approved anonymous descriptor.
- [ ] Industry and client type.
- [ ] Original problem.
- [ ] Previous/current workflow.
- [ ] Intended users and important assumptions.
- [ ] Product decision and why it was made.
- [ ] What was deliberately excluded from the first release.
- [ ] Product built and technical solution at an appropriate level.
- [ ] Founder’s actual role.
- [ ] Team’s actual role.
- [ ] Screenshots with confidential/test data removed.
- [ ] Descriptive alt text for every screenshot.
- [ ] Measurable results with timeframe, source, baseline, and verification note.
- [ ] Client quotation, if approved.
- [ ] Named client representative/role/company, if approved.
- [ ] Written client permission defining what may be published.
- [ ] Permission for website, social previews, organic social, and paid ads separately where relevant.
- [ ] Legal/confidentiality review.
- [ ] Publication status (`draft` or `published`).
- [ ] Review/expiry date and owner.

Do not convert qualitative feedback into a numeric metric. Do not publish drafts, unverified results, confidential interface data, or client quotations without permission. If fewer than two projects are approved and published, retain the capability-led alternative section and do not show fake filters.

## Testimonials

For each testimonial:

- [ ] Exact approved quotation.
- [ ] Client name.
- [ ] Client role.
- [ ] Company.
- [ ] Photograph or company logo, if permission covers it.
- [ ] LinkedIn profile or company website where appropriate.
- [ ] Related project/case-study slug.
- [ ] Written permission to publish the quote and attribution.
- [ ] Permission for paid advertising if it may appear in creative.
- [ ] `permissionConfirmed` status.
- [ ] `published` status.
- [ ] Approval date, expiry/review date, and owner.

Hide the section when there are no approved, published testimonials. Do not add star ratings, aggregate-review numbers, or review structured data without genuine source data that meets platform requirements.

## Product and interface imagery

- [ ] Approved screenshots for each case study.
- [ ] Confidential data, personal data, API keys, internal URLs, and customer names removed or explicitly permitted.
- [ ] Device frames/crops do not obscure important content or imply unsupported platforms.
- [ ] Image dimensions and high-density variants are suitable for responsive delivery.
- [ ] Descriptive alt text supplied or reviewed.
- [ ] Copyright/license and client permission recorded.
- [ ] Social-sharing image reflects the real site and contains only approved text/proof.
- [ ] Favicon and brand icon approved.

Abstract CSS/product-workflow visuals do not require customer imagery, but their labels and claims still need content review.

## Pricing and commercial details

- [ ] US Blueprint price confirmed: “From $2,500.”
- [ ] US Focused MVP price confirmed: “From $10,000.”
- [ ] US Custom Product price confirmed: “From $25,000.”
- [ ] UK Blueprint price confirmed: “From £2,000.”
- [ ] UK Focused MVP price confirmed: “From £8,000.”
- [ ] UK Custom Product price confirmed: “From £20,000.”
- [ ] Package inclusions/exclusions reviewed by delivery leadership.
- [ ] “From” qualification and final-scope caveats remain visible.
- [ ] Blueprint (1–2 weeks), MVP (approximately 6–10 weeks), and scoped custom timelines are operationally accurate.
- [ ] Thirty days of MVP defect support is consistent with the agreement template.
- [ ] Code ownership statement matches the actual project agreement.
- [ ] Free Product Clarity Call boundaries are correct.
- [ ] No checkout, discounts, strikethrough pricing, guarantees, or artificial urgency have been introduced.

Market prices are manually configured. Do not replace them with a live exchange-rate conversion.

## Page-copy review

- [ ] Homepage headline, supporting copy, trust microcopy, services, process, audience-fit, FAQ, pricing, founder/team, and final CTA reviewed.
- [ ] Services page remains outcome-focused rather than a technology directory.
- [ ] Process page accurately describes participants, inputs, outputs, decisions, and client responsibilities.
- [ ] About page founder story, company purpose, in-house model, principles, availability, and values are factual.
- [ ] Work page and every published case study contain only verified material.
- [ ] UK campaign page matches UK prices, availability, and booking.
- [ ] US campaign page matches US prices, availability, and booking.
- [ ] Application field/options and qualification-result copy match sales operations.
- [ ] Email templates and internal notification recipients are correct.
- [ ] All navigation, CTA, button, form, success, error, empty-state, 404, and unavailable-provider copy is final.
- [ ] No `FOUNDER_NAME`, `example.com`, sample phone number, starter title, draft label, or other prominent placeholder is visible in production.
- [ ] Claims such as “in-house team,” “founder-led,” services offered, geographic coverage, and support periods are confirmed.

## Legal, privacy, and consent materials

Have a qualified professional review these for the real legal entity, services, markets, vendors, and data flows:

- [ ] Privacy notice.
- [ ] Terms of use/service.
- [ ] Cookie notice and category descriptions.
- [ ] Necessary-contact consent wording.
- [ ] Separate optional marketing-consent wording.
- [ ] Lead retention period and deletion process.
- [ ] Data-subject/contact process.
- [ ] Controller/contact details.
- [ ] UK/US and cross-border transfer language where applicable.
- [ ] Advertising, analytics, `_fbp`/`_fbc`, and hashed-identifier disclosures.
- [ ] Processor/vendor list: hosting, database, email, anti-spam, booking, analytics, Meta, and CRM.
- [ ] NDA and intellectual-property FAQ wording.
- [ ] Accessibility/contact statement if published.

Generated placeholders are not legal advice and must not claim guaranteed compliance.

## Service credentials and operational ownership

- [ ] Production Sites project, domain, and environment owner.
- [ ] Lead storage project/database and migration owner.
- [ ] Supabase service-role key or signed CRM webhook credentials.
- [ ] Resend account, verified domain, sender, and internal recipient.
- [ ] Production Turnstile site/secret keys and allowed hostnames.
- [ ] UK, US, and fallback booking destinations.
- [ ] Meta Business assets, Pixel/dataset, CAPI token, and test-event access.
- [ ] Google Analytics property and measurement ID.
- [ ] Consent/privacy owner.
- [ ] Lead review owner and response-time expectation.
- [ ] Incident/error notification owner.
- [ ] Retention/deletion owner.
- [ ] Case-study/testimonial permission owner.

## Final approval record

- [ ] Product/delivery owner approved scope and pricing.
- [ ] Founder approved identity, story, imagery, and claims.
- [ ] Sales owner approved form, scoring, routing, emails, and booking journey.
- [ ] Privacy/legal reviewer approved policies and consent behavior.
- [ ] Marketing owner approved creative, attribution, analytics, and Meta payloads.
- [ ] Technical owner confirmed all replacement content is deployed, optimized, accessible, and free of sensitive metadata.
- [ ] Approval dates and responsible names are stored outside the public repository.
